Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 · Last Updated: October 19, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and web storage technologies are, how OmniTools ("we", "us", or "our") uses them on https://example.com, and your choices regarding their management.

1. What are Cookies and Web Storage?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device by a web server when you visit a website. Web Storage (including HTML5 localStorage) is a modern browser mechanism that allows web applications to store key-value data locally within the user's browser with greater capacity and security than traditional HTTP cookies.

2. How We Use Cookies & Local Storage

OmniTools operates with a strict data-minimization philosophy. We do not use cookies to collect personal identity details or track your keystrokes. Below is a complete breakdown of the storage technologies utilized on our website:

A. Strictly Necessary & Functional Local Storage

These storage items are essential for the website to function properly and provide you with a customized user experience:

  • omnitools_theme: Stores your chosen interface theme (either 'dark' or 'light'). This allows your display preference to persist when navigating between different tool pages and on subsequent visits, preventing an unpleasant visual flash of unstyled content.
  • omnitools_cookie_consent: Records whether you have accepted or customized our cookie consent banner ('all' or 'essential') so the banner is not redundantly displayed on every page load.

B. Third-Party Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

To maintain OmniTools as a 100% free resource for students, writers, and developers, we may partner with automated third-party ad networks, notably Google AdSense.

When enabled, Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their visits to OmniTools and other sites on the web. These cookies collect non-personally identifiable technical identifiers such as browser type, approximate geolocation, and device category to:

  • Measure advertising impressions and performance.
  • Prevent the same ad from showing repetitively to the same user.
  • Deliver relevant contextual or personalized ads where consented.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's official Ad Settings page: https://www.google.com/settings/ads or through the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out portal at http://www.aboutads.info/choices/.

3. How to Manage and Disable Cookies in Your Browser

Most modern web browsers allow you to control cookie preferences, view stored data, and delete cookies at will. You can modify your browser settings to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being set:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Prevent cross-site tracking / Block all cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.

Please note: Disabling essential local storage will reset your light/dark theme preference to system defaults on each visit.

4. Updates to this Cookie Policy

We may update our Cookie Policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please revisit this Cookie Policy periodically to stay informed.

5. Questions and Contact

If you have any questions regarding our use of cookies or local storage technologies, please email us at support@example.com or visit our Contact Us page.
