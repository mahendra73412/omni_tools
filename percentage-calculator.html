Mastering Percentage Calculations

Percentages (from the Latin per centum, meaning "by the hundred") express numbers as a fraction of 100. They are foundational to personal finance, business budgeting, restaurant tipping, retail discounting, and academic grading.

Formulas Breakdown

  • Portion Calculation (What is P% of X?):
    Result = (P / 100) × X
    Example: To calculate an 8% sales tax on a \$120 purchase: (8 / 100) × 120 = \$9.60 tax.
  • Fraction to Percentage (X is what % of Y?):
    Result = (X / Y) × 100
    Example: If a student scores 42 out of 50 points on an exam: (42 / 50) × 100 = 84%.
  • Percentage Change (Increase / Decrease):
    Change (%) = ((Final Value - Initial Value) / |Initial Value|) × 100
    Example: If website visitors increase from 5,000 to 7,500: ((7,500 - 5,000) / 5,000) × 100 = +50% growth.
  • Applying Markups and Discounts:
    New Price = Base Price ± (Base Price × Discount Rate)
    Example: A \$200 jacket on a 25% clearance discount costs \$200 - \$50 = \$150.
